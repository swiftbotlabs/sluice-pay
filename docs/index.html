          Where do I find my Solana address?
          1. Open Phantom and tap Receive.
          2. Find Solana in the list and tap Copy.
          3. Come back and tap Paste.
          1. Open Solflare and tap your wallet name at the top.
          2. Tap Copy address.
          3. Come back and tap Paste.

          Use a wallet you control instead. Exchange deposit addresses can change or need a memo, and settling into Kamino needs your own wallet. Phantom and Solflare are free and take a few minutes to set up.

          Never paste these
          • Your recovery phrase (12 or 24 words) or a private key. Sluice never asks for them.
          • An Ethereum-style address that starts with 0x.
          • A token's own address, like USDC's. It has to be your wallet.

              Invoices that earn from the first second

              Payment rails leave money idle until someone moves it. With Sluice a customer on Base can pay an invoice and, in one atomic Solana transaction run through Aurora Intents Connect, the USDC is supplied to Kamino and the yield-bearing cUSDC lands in the merchant's own token account. The payer's account never holds the position, and the order's reference travels with the deposit, so the payment is findable from Solana alone.

              1. BasePayer signs once, sends USDC
              2. NEAR IntentsRoutes it to Solana
              3. KaminoSupplies USDC, mints cUSDC
              4. MerchantOwns the position

              For APIs and agents

              The same rail works without a browser. A Sluice API answers 402 Payment Required with its price in USDC on Solana. The buyer pays from any supported chain and calls again. The server delivers exactly once, only after it has read the USDC credit on Solana, and returns a signed receipt.

              A hosted one is live. Ask it for its price:

              curl -i https://sluice-api.swiftbotlab.workers.dev/api/report/9cYFzrmHRcsYzLhAWsNemzCy75ZhETn2px4MS8oDDReR

              Then create an order with your own refund address, send the exact amount it quotes from your own wallet, and call again with X-Sluice-Order. The receipt key is published at /.well-known/sluice.json. Every step, with the headless buyer that checks the quote against NEAR Intents' public record before it pays:

              $ python3 cli/sluice_buy.py https://sluice-api.swiftbotlab.workers.dev/api/report/<address> \
    --pay base:USDC --key-file payer.key --max-usd 0.30
