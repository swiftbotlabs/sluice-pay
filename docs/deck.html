Sluice

Get paid in USDC on Solana, by anyone, from any chain.

The problem

A Solana merchant loses every customer whose money sits somewhere else.

  • Customers must bridge, find gas on the right chain, and hope nothing breaks
  • Merchants get "paid" by a provider's status message, not by a balance they can check
  • When a payment comes up short, someone has to chase it by hand

Sluice

  • Pay links signed by the merchant's Solana wallet
  • 202 assets on 34 networks, live from Aurora; paid live from Base, Arbitrum and Optimism
  • Nothing counts until Solana shows it

A page that never expires

One permanent address per network, for tips, top-ups and a QR on the counter.

  • Any amount, any time, from whatever the customer holds
  • No quote, no wallet connect, no form
  • Each address is checked against Aurora's record before it is shown
  • Fixed prices stay on signed links, which keep refunds

Live on mainnet: on 4 Nov a plain 1.10 USDC transfer on Base reached the merchant on Solana as 1.089777 USDC, 14 seconds after Aurora saw it.

Invoices that earn

The payer's money lands in the merchant's own yield position, in one atomic transaction.

  1. BasePayer signs once, sends USDC
  2. NEAR IntentsRoutes it to Solana
  3. KaminoSupplies USDC, mints cUSDC
  4. MerchantOwns the position

Two legs through Intents Connect: the cross-chain swap, then a call into a Solana program, with the order reference attached. Live on mainnet both ways: 0.124074 cUSDC to the merchant from one signature on Base, then redeemed by the merchant for 0.150041 USDC.

You should not have to trust us

  • Links: amount, address and how it settles are signed. One changed character and the checkout refuses
  • Signatures: the page decodes the exact Solana transaction before your wallet opens, and refuses anything else
  • Payments: anyone can re-check thirteen mainnet runs from the browser, no server involved
  • Orders: a reference account makes each payment findable from Solana alone

Beyond people: APIs and agents

An API answers 402. The buyer pays from any chain. The server delivers exactly once, after Solana confirms, with a signed receipt.

The buyer does not trust the seller: it cross-checks the deposit address with public NEAR Intents data before paying.
